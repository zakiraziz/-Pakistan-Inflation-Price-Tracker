What an alert does and does not tell you

  • Does: flag a single-week move large enough to be worth a look, and point at the exact observation.
  • Does not: explain why it moved. Fresh produce legitimately swings with the season, so a 10% onion move is not the same signal as a 10% electricity move.
  • Thresholds are per week. Anything sustained shows up in the trend and volatility columns on the item page.
